## Melon Web Apps & Tools 🍈
Here are all the web apps and websites that follow the principles of the Melon project, coded entirely in HTML, CSS and JS, without the need of those big ass dependencies or that kind of things.

Seriously, no image.ts with 800 sub-dependencies written by a guy who forgot how modulo works. No npm run build that takes longer than staring at a boiling egg. No huge ass node_modules folder that is probably making a complot against your SSD, and most importantly, you download the html and run it yourself, which means bye bye Electron. Vanilla JS runs faster anyway, stop coping. It doesn't track your soul, your IP, or what you had for breakfast:

* **[Melange](Melange-EN.html)** - A visual project board featuring spatial cards, to-do lists, color swatches, columns, documents and smooth curve connectors. Zero cloud tracking, zero stupid frameworks, and most importantly, zero Node.js. All at the amazing and only 33KB of size. How the fuck did you do this, Cotton? Melon doing its magic. Aviable both in [Spanish](Melange.html) and [English](Melange-EN.html) 

* **[Memory](Memory.html)** - A thought collector and organizer for people who forget stuff often. Made simple and minimalistic to ensure as little friction as possible between you and the thought you want to record. Everything that happens stays between you and Memory. Efficient, completely Melon, lightweight, free and open source, tho it could improve.

Your CPU fan can finally take a vacation. You're welcome.
